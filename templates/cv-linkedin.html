📄 CV & LinkedIn

Learn how to create a professional CV and build a strong LinkedIn presence that represents your skills.

Why Is a Good CV Important?

Your CV is often one of the first things an employer sees when considering you for an opportunity. A good CV clearly presents your education, skills, experience, projects, certificates and achievements.

What You'll Learn

• How to structure a professional CV
• Writing a strong personal profile
• Presenting your education
• Listing relevant skills
• Adding projects and experience
• Presenting certificates
• Avoiding common CV mistakes
• Tailoring your CV to opportunities

Building Your LinkedIn Profile

LinkedIn can help you establish a professional identity before graduation. You'll learn how to create your profile, write an effective headline and About section, showcase projects, add skills and certifications, and connect professionally with other people.

Building Your Professional Brand

Learn how to present your achievements and learning experiences professionally. You can showcase projects, certifications, volunteering, leadership activities and other experiences that demonstrate your development.

For Students

You don't have to wait until graduation to start building your professional profile. Students can use their education, school projects, skills, certifications and relevant activities to begin developing their professional presence.
