🚀 Career Development

Learn how to understand your career interests, develop useful skills and prepare for future opportunities.

What Is Career Development?

Career development is the process of understanding your interests, building useful skills, gaining experience and preparing yourself for future education and work opportunities.

Discover Your Career Direction

Learn how to identify your interests, strengths, skills and areas you would like to explore. Understanding yourself can help you make more informed decisions about your education and career.

Skills Employers Value

• Communication
• Teamwork
• Problem solving
• Critical thinking
• Time management
• Leadership
• Digital skills
• Adaptability
• Professionalism

Building Experience

You can begin gaining useful experience before graduation through school projects, volunteering, internships, competitions, personal projects and other relevant activities.

Preparing for Opportunities

Learn how to search for internships, graduate opportunities and other career experiences. You'll also learn the importance of preparing your CV, building your professional profile and developing skills that match your goals.

Creating a Career Plan

Set realistic goals and break them into smaller steps. A career plan can help you identify the skills, experiences and learning opportunities you want to pursue.
