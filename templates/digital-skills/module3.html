🌐 Internet & Web Basics

The internet is one of the most important tools for communication, education, research, and work. Understanding how it works helps you use digital technology effectively.

🎯 What You Will Learn

  • What the internet is
  • What a website is
  • How web browsers work
  • What a search engine does
  • Basic internet safety practices

🌍 What Is the Internet?

The internet is a worldwide network that connects computers, phones, servers, and other devices so they can communicate and share information.

People use the internet for activities such as learning, communication, entertainment, research, shopping, and work.

🖥️ What Is a Website?

A website is a collection of related web pages that can be accessed through the internet using a web browser.

Websites can provide information, services, educational resources, applications, and many other types of content.

🔎 What Is a Search Engine?

A search engine helps users find information on the internet by entering words or phrases related to what they are looking for.

Good search queries are usually specific and describe exactly what you want to find.

🌐 Web Browsers

A web browser is software used to access and view websites.

  • Google Chrome
  • Microsoft Edge
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Safari

🔗 Web Addresses

Websites have addresses that tell your browser where to find a particular website or web page. These addresses are commonly called URLs.

🛡️ Basic Internet Safety

Using the internet safely is an important digital skill.

  • Use strong and unique passwords.
  • Do not share your passwords with other people.
  • Be careful when clicking unfamiliar links.
  • Check information before trusting or sharing it.
  • Keep your devices and software updated.
  • Be careful when sharing personal information online.
💡 Key Point: The internet connects devices around the world, while web browsers allow you to access websites and online information.

📝 Module Summary

The internet provides access to information and digital services around the world. Understanding websites, browsers, search engines, URLs, and basic online safety will help you become a more confident digital user.
