🔐 Digital Safety, Cybersecurity & Responsible Technology Use

Being able to use technology is important, but knowing how to use it safely and responsibly is equally important. This final module introduces basic cybersecurity, online safety, privacy, and responsible use of technology.

🎯 What You Will Learn

  • What cybersecurity means
  • How to create stronger passwords
  • How to recognize suspicious messages
  • Why privacy matters online
  • How to protect your accounts
  • Responsible use of technology

🛡️ What Is Cybersecurity?

Cybersecurity refers to protecting computers, devices, accounts, networks, and information from unauthorized access and other digital threats.

Examples of things worth protecting:

🔑 Account passwords
📧 Email accounts
📱 Mobile devices
📄 Personal documents
💳 Financial information

🔑 Strong Passwords

Passwords help protect your online accounts. A good password should be difficult for other people to guess.

  • Use passwords that are difficult to guess.
  • Avoid using the same password everywhere.
  • Do not share your passwords with other people.
  • Use multi-factor authentication when available.

🎣 Recognizing Suspicious Messages

Some online messages are designed to trick people into giving away information or clicking unsafe links. This type of scam is commonly known as phishing.

Be cautious when a message creates pressure, asks for sensitive information, or comes from an unexpected sender.

💡 Remember: If a message seems suspicious, do not rush to click a link or provide personal information. Verify the message through a trusted source first.

🔒 Protecting Your Privacy

Personal information should be handled carefully online. Think about what information you share and who can access it.

  • Review privacy settings on your accounts.
  • Be careful when sharing personal information.
  • Only give information to trustworthy services.
  • Log out of accounts on shared devices.

📱 Protecting Your Devices

Keep your devices and applications updated. Updates can contain security improvements and fixes for known problems.

Use screen locks and avoid leaving your devices unattended where other people could access them.

⚖️ Responsible Technology Use

Technology should be used responsibly and respectfully. Digital responsibility includes respecting other people's privacy, following rules, and thinking about the consequences of what you post or share.

  • Respect other people's privacy.
  • Do not spread false information deliberately.
  • Respect copyright and ownership of digital content.
  • Use technology for productive and appropriate purposes.
  • Think before posting or sharing content.

💼 Digital Safety at Work

Digital safety is also important in the workplace. Employees may handle company accounts, documents, customer information, and other sensitive data.

Following security policies and reporting suspicious activity can help protect an organization.

📝 Module Summary

Digital skills are not only about using technology. You also need to understand how to protect your information, recognize suspicious activity, respect privacy, and use technology responsibly.

🎯 Key Point: Stay alert, protect your accounts, respect other people online, and think before you click, share, or post.
