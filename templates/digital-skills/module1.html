Introduction to Digital Skills

Digital skills are the abilities needed to use computers, smartphones, software, websites, and other digital technologies effectively and responsibly.

🎯 What You Will Learn

  • What digital skills are
  • Why digital skills are important
  • Examples of common digital skills
  • How digital skills can help with education and careers

💻 What Are Digital Skills?

Digital skills are practical abilities that help you use digital devices and technology to find information, communicate, create content, solve problems, and complete tasks.

🌍 Why Are Digital Skills Important?

Technology is used in education, businesses, engineering, healthcare, communication, entertainment, and many other areas. Developing digital skills can therefore make it easier to learn, work, communicate, and adapt to new technologies.

🧠 Examples of Digital Skills

  • Using a computer or smartphone
  • Searching for information online
  • Using email
  • Creating documents
  • Working with spreadsheets
  • Creating presentations
  • Using cloud storage
  • Practising good online safety
💡 Key Point: Digital skills are not only about knowing how to use a device. They also include using technology safely, responsibly, and effectively to accomplish real-world tasks.

📝 Module Summary

Digital skills help people use technology effectively in everyday life, education, and the workplace.
