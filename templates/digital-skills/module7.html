🌐 Internet & Online Safety

The internet is an important tool for learning, communication, research, entertainment, and work. However, using the internet safely requires good digital habits.

🎯 What You Will Learn

  • What the internet is
  • How websites and web browsers work
  • How to search for information effectively
  • How to identify suspicious online activity
  • How to protect your personal information
  • How to behave responsibly online

🌍 What Is the Internet?

The internet is a worldwide network that connects computers, phones, servers, and other devices so they can communicate and share information.

People use the internet to access websites, send messages, attend online classes, conduct research, and use many different digital services.

🔎 Searching for Information

Search engines help users find information on the web. To get better results, use specific keywords that describe what you are looking for.

Example:

Instead of searching:
"Excel"

Try:
"Excel beginner formulas tutorial"

🔐 Protecting Personal Information

Personal information should be handled carefully online. Avoid sharing sensitive information with people or websites you do not trust.

  • Use strong, unique passwords.
  • Do not share passwords with other people.
  • Be careful when entering personal information.
  • Review privacy settings on online accounts.
  • Log out of accounts when using shared computers.

🎣 Recognizing Phishing

Phishing is an attempt to trick people into giving away information such as passwords or account details. Phishing messages may pretend to come from a legitimate company, school, bank, or other organization.

⚠️ Warning signs: Be cautious of unexpected messages that create urgency, request sensitive information, or contain suspicious links.

🔑 Strong Passwords

A strong password is difficult for other people to guess. Different accounts should ideally use different passwords.

When available, multi-factor authentication can provide an additional layer of account security.

📰 Checking Online Information

Not everything published online is accurate. Before trusting important information, consider where it came from and whether other reliable sources support it.

  • Check the source.
  • Check the date.
  • Compare information with reliable sources.
  • Be careful with sensational claims.

🤝 Responsible Online Behaviour

Good digital citizenship means treating other people respectfully and using technology responsibly.

  • Communicate respectfully.
  • Think before posting.
  • Do not spread false information.
  • Respect other people's privacy.
  • Report harmful or suspicious activity when appropriate.

💼 Online Safety at Work

Digital safety is also important in professional environments. Employees may handle company accounts, documents, customer information, and other important data.

📝 Module Summary

Safe internet use involves protecting personal information, recognizing suspicious messages, using strong passwords, checking information carefully, and behaving responsibly online.

🎯 Key Point: Think before you click, share, download, or post.
