💻 Digital Skills

Develop practical computer and digital skills that can help you succeed in school and future work.

Why Digital Skills Matter

Technology is part of almost every modern career. Developing strong digital skills can help you work more efficiently, communicate professionally and adapt to new technologies.

What You'll Learn

• Computer fundamentals
• Internet and web skills
• Online research
• Microsoft Office and productivity tools
• Document creation and formatting
• Presentations
• Spreadsheets and basic data handling
• Digital communication
• Online collaboration

Internet & Online Skills

Learn how to search for reliable information, communicate professionally online, use online learning platforms and work with digital resources.

Productivity Skills

Learn how to create well-organized documents, presentations and spreadsheets that can be useful for school projects, applications and future work.

Digital Safety

Understand basic online safety practices, including protecting your accounts, creating strong passwords, recognizing suspicious messages and being responsible with personal information online.

Who Is This For?

This course is suitable for students and beginners who want to become more confident using computers, online tools and digital technology.
