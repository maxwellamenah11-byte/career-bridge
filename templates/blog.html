📄 How to Build Your First CV

Your first CV doesn't need to contain years of professional experience. You can highlight your education, relevant skills, school projects, certifications, volunteering and other experiences.

Keep your CV clear, organized and relevant to the opportunity you're applying for.

💼 Why Students Should Start LinkedIn Early

LinkedIn can help students begin developing a professional identity before graduation.

You can showcase your education, projects, skills, certifications and achievements while gradually building a professional network.

💻 Skills to Learn Before Graduation

Academic knowledge is important, but practical skills can also help students prepare for the workplace.

Consider developing skills such as communication, digital literacy, problem solving, teamwork, programming and professional writing.

🎓 How to Prepare for Internships

Start by identifying opportunities related to your interests and field of study.

Prepare a professional CV, develop relevant skills, research the organization and practise communicating your experience and interests.
