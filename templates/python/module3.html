Arithmetic Operators

Python can perform mathematical calculations using operators.

addition = 10 + 5
subtraction = 10 - 5
multiplication = 10 * 5
division = 10 / 5

print(addition)
print(subtraction)
print(multiplication)
print(division)

Comparison Operators

age = 16

print(age == 16)
print(age > 18)
print(age < 18)

Logical Operators

age = 20

print(age >= 18 and age <= 30)
